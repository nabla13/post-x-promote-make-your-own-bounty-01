Entry for make.fun's "Promote make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post on X" bounty ($250, top 50 paid). The entry is one post on X that quotes or links make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post, with a photo of its own attached; places go by the post's replies + reposts + quotes. `POST.json` names the post's address, and `main.ts` hands it to the gate by copying it to `/output/POST.json`. make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win — anyone can now make a bounty of their own there: describe any task, set the reward and the entry fee, and keep a share of every fee people pay to enter.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
